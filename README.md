# Madhouse

A VR interactive game partially inspired by *Alice in Wonderland*. The player follows a strange journey through a series of surreal environments and encounters different challenges along the way.

## Story

Madhouse takes parts of the *Alice in Wonderland* narrative and reimagines them as a VR experience.

The player enters a strange world where familiar elements from the story appear in new environments. As they move through the game, they encounter different situations and mini puzzles that gradually lead them through the experience. Each puzzle is part of the player's journey and helps them move forward into the next part of the world.

The game focuses less on recreating the original story exactly and more on giving the player the feeling of exploring a world where the environment and its rules are constantly changing.

## Gameplay

The player explores the environment in VR and completes a series of mini puzzles to progress through the game. Objects and elements in the environment can be interacted with using the VR controllers.

- Explore a surreal 3D environment
- Interact with objects using VR controllers
- Solve mini puzzles
- Progress through different parts of the story
- Discover Wonderland-inspired environments and interactions

## Controls

### Keyboard

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` | Move forward |
| `A` | Move left |
| `S` | Move backward |
| `D` | Move right |

### VR

Use the VR controllers to look around, move, and interact with objects.

## Built With

- **Unity:** game development and VR implementation
- **Unity HDRP:** rendering, lighting, and visual effects
- **Unity XR Interaction Toolkit:** VR interaction and controller input
- **Blender:** 3D modeling and environment assets
- **C#:** gameplay and interaction scripting

## Project

Madhouse was created as a VR game project exploring interactive storytelling, 3D environments, puzzle design, and immersive interaction.

The project combines elements of *Alice in Wonderland* with original environments and gameplay to create a new interpretation of the story rather than a direct recreation.
